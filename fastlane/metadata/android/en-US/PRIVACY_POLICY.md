# VentoyDroid Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-26

VentoyDroid is an Android app that creates bootable multiboot USB sticks
(using the open-source Ventoy boot manager) directly from a phone, over USB
OTG. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Data the app processes

**Data stored on your device**
- ISO files you select are read from your storage and written to the USB
  stick. They are never uploaded anywhere.
- App settings (e.g., whether the battery-optimization dialog was shown) are
  stored locally on the device.

**Information collected by third parties (advertising)**

VentoyDroid is ad-supported and uses **Google AdMob** (Google Ireland Ltd /
Google LLC). When ads are displayed, Google may collect and process:

- Device identifiers (e.g., advertising ID), device model, OS version
- Approximate location (country/city level, derived from IP)
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
- App usage data related to ad delivery (e.g., whether an ad was shown)

Google's use of this information is governed by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Users in the
EEA/UK are shown a consent form (Google UMP) before personalized ads may be
served; you may withdraw or change consent via your device's ad settings or
by clearing the app's data.

**Data NOT collected**
- We do not collect names, emails, contacts, photos, or precise location.
- We do not read, upload, or share the contents of your ISO files.
- The app has no analytics SDKs other than the advertising SDK above.

## Permissions used

- **USB host access**: to read/write the USB stick (core function).
- **Internet / network state**: required by the ad SDK only.
- **Notifications**: to show install progress when the app is backgrounded.
- **Wake lock / battery-optimization exemption**: so installs are not
  interrupted mid-write; used only during an active install.
